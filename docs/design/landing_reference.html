    The rules

    Assumed

    These are the thresholds the example on the left is judged by. Nothing about them has been tuned on real data yet.

    • BUILDsuccess rate at least 80%, gross margin at least 60%, runway at least 12 months, retry tax at most 35%.
    • KILLsuccess rate under 50%, or gross margin under 20%, or runway under 6 months.
    • PIVOTanything in between.
